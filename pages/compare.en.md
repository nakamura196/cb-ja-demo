## Search comparison

The same catalogue of 34 items, searched two ways.

- **Original CollectionBuilder**: the built-in search (lunr with its default settings). Its English-oriented pre-processing treats kanji and kana as punctuation and strips them, so even an exact title returns 0 results.
- **CJK-ready**: the search added in this demo. It indexes text in overlapping two-character pieces and treats old and new character forms (圖/図, 學/学), katakana and hiragana, and full- and half-width characters as the same.
