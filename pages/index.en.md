58 items with Japanese, Classical Chinese and Korean titles, all published on the [University of Tokyo Digital Archives Portal](https://da.dl.itc.u-tokyo.ac.jp/portal/): 34 rare books from the Library for Agricultural and Life Sciences, 12 volumes of the Annals of the Joseon Dynasty, and 12 Korean-language items from the Ogura Collection.
Two of them (a bird's-eye view and a botanical album) also appear as samples served from this site with static IIIF.

The old form 圖 is found by the modern 図, and Traditional 實錄 by Simplified 实录.
Korean titles can be found from any part of the title.
See [the search comparison](compare.html) for how this differs from the original CollectionBuilder.
